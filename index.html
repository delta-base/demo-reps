Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about choosing the right AI Role Play platform

Can I try a conversation on this website?+

Yes. Select any scenario for a short scripted demo. You will set a baseline and intention, choose or type responses, review feedback tied to your choices, and try again. Responses and feedback in this website demo are scripted; there is no live AI or open-ended voice conversation connected.

How is the DEMO different from the paid simulation platform?+

The DEMO offers short, scripted practice through guided responses or free-text conversation.

The paid simulation platform offers a wider range of detailed scenarios and personalises your goals and specific practice intentions through ongoing assessments. It analyses your conversation transcript against structured judging criteria, grounding each debrief in evidence and highlighting missed opportunities.

Coaching supports behavioural change through COM-B diagnosis—examining capability, opportunity and motivation—and a mechanism hypothesis: a working explanation of what may be driving a behaviour and what could help you change it. These insights guide your next practice goals as you progress.

How long does it take to see results with REPS?+

Most teams see improvements within 30 days. Sales leaders report reps ramping 40–67% faster, increased meeting book rates, and higher confidence on discovery calls within the first month of consistent practice.

What is REPS?+

REPS is a concept for an AI-powered practice platform where professionals rehearse important conversations in a safe learning setting. It starts with negotiations for founders and sales professionals, bringing assessment, focused knowhow, simulation, coaching, and reflection into one learning loop.

Who is it designed for?+

Founders preparing for investor and partner conversations, and sales professionals dealing with price pressure, objections, and procurement. The shared aim is to practise clear, curious, and constructive negotiation before the real conversation.

How does the AI-Coach fit into the learning loop?+

The intended REPS experience separates the negotiation counterpart from the coaching role. After practice, the coach relates your intention to evidence from the conversation, identifies a useful behaviour and a missed opportunity, and suggests a focused next attempt. Both demo modes illustrate this with locally simulated feedback.

What does progress actually mean?+

Progress means using a target behaviour more consistently in relevant situations. REPS combines self-reflection with practice evidence. The charts here are illustrative, and the short demo is not a validated assessment or a predictor of real-world negotiation results.

What happens to my demo responses?+

The demo keeps your choices in this page's memory while it is open. It does not send them to an AI service or save them as a learner record. Refreshing clears them. The “Reset this exercise” option clears the current demo history so you can begin again.

Why only three turns in the demo?+

Three turns make the behavioural pattern visible quickly. Full simulations can run for 10–20 minutes and adapt to the learner’s responses.

Can scenarios be personalised by role or sector?+

Yes. The same behavioural capability can be practised with different commercial contexts, buyer roles, constraints, pressure tactics and difficulty levels.

How is REPS different from other AI Roleplay platforms?+

FullyRamped covers the full sales cycle—not just cold calling. It supports discovery calls, product demos, deal prep, certifications, and ongoing enablement for true everboarding. Plus, you can customize scenarios yourself without needing implementation services or partner coaches.

What's your pricing model and contract length?+

For B2C, we have a discounted pricing page.

For B2B, we offer flexible pricing based on team size that scales with you, with options that let you pilot the platform with confidence. Book a demo to discuss what works best for your organization.

How quickly can my team get started?+

Most teams are up and running within days, not weeks. We'll support you through onboarding and teach your users how to create simulations. We are responsive to help you get started fast, and the platform is designed for self-serve customizations.

Does REPS work for technical sales and demo calls?+

Absolutely. Unlike cold-calling-only platforms, FullyRamped excels at complex sales conversations including technical demos, discovery calls with multiple stakeholders, and deal-specific preparation.
